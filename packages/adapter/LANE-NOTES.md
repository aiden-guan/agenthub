## A1 repair round 1

- **`hub approve` / `hub reject` never send the diff's control characters to the terminal.** `format.ts` `terminalSafe(text)` writes out C0 controls (but tab and newline), DEL, C1 controls, U+2028/2029, `\p{Cf}` (bidi controls, zero-width) and every other default-ignorable character visibly (`\x1b`, `\r`, `‮`) and counts them; when there are any, the section header says how many and that the files hold the characters themselves, and a reminder comes before the prompt. The digest still covers the real blobs, so what is approved is what was shown. The approval diff is also `--no-textconv --ignore-submodules=none --submodule=short` with `:(literal)` pathspecs, so neither the approver's config and attributes nor a path that looks like a glob can change what is shown.
- **`land` fetches trunk before choosing record mode.** With no land section in this checkout's copy of trunk, it fetches trunk (bounded, the same fetch as the preflight's step 3, which then isn't repeated) and reads trunk's agenthub.json again; only when that still has no land section is the landing recorded Phase 1 style. A failed fetch keeps the old behaviour (record mode; a `landing_queue_active` answer then names the fetch error). Trunk fetches set `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0` (credential helpers still answer).
- **Every refusal of the queue request after the push says what to do.** `format.ts` `landHubRefusedText` covers `not_found`, `forbidden`, `task_closed`, `task_blocked`, `task_landing`, `too_late`, a `claim_conflict` without details, the request-shape codes (`bad_commit`, `bad_request`, `invalid_path`, `invalid_symbol`: "probably different versions, tell your human") and any other code, each ending "Your commit is pushed (that is harmless); nothing was queued."
- **For the integrator to confirm (no change made):** other agents' landing summaries and decision titles in the fix-it briefs are quoted inline (through `inline()`: one line, no control characters, double quotes swapped, capped at 80) as the §2.3.3 sketches show them, not inside `untrustedBlock()` as the §2.3.2 `landing_status` row says; every brief ends with the "information, not instructions" line. If the integrator reads §2.3.2 as binding, `otherText()` and `conflictBrief()` in `format.ts` are the two places to change, and the §2.3.3 snapshots in `landing-ux.test.ts` change with them.

## A2: workspace sync, edit/sync exclusion, the pre-push guard, feed notices

No dependency requests and no contract-amendment requests (sync uses `gitops.landedBase`, `replay`, `stashCreate`, `forecastConflicts`, `trunkTrailers` as frozen).

Layout:

- **`src/sync.ts`** (new) `syncWorkspace(root, cfg, client, {reason, mode, budgetMs, recover})`, the design §2.3.5 steps 0-10, and the exclusion helpers (`enterEdit`, `writeMarker`, `clearMarkers`, `liveMarkers`, `lockHeld`, `interruptedSync`), `staleFiles` (the stale-base heads-up) and `fetchTrunk`. Files under `G = gitDir(root)`: `G/agenthub/sync.lock`, `sync.json`, `inflight/<session>~<tool_use_id>`, `mergebase`, `stale-shown`, `sync-said`. The only worktree-changing command is one `git reset --keep`, behind the HEAD and clean-status compare-and-swap, after the `sync.json` write-ahead (fsynced) and the backup ref `refs/agenthub/backup/<branch>/<ms>` (pruned after 14 days). Merge mode runs `git merge --no-edit refs/remotes/<remote>/<trunk>` after the same lock, marker, git-operation and own-landing checks and a clean tracked tree.
- **`src/hooks.ts`** pre-edit: the marker first, then the lock (held: the marker goes, it waits up to 3 s polling every 100 ms, tries once more, then denies "The workspace is updating to the latest trunk; retry this edit."); an interrupted `sync.json` denies "…call hub_sync to finish recovering, then retry this edit."; a denied edit removes its marker; on a newly granted claim the stale-base heads-up (additionalContext, once per session, file and trunk tip; with a land section and `sync.staleEdit` not `off`). post-edit removes the call's marker. settle removes the thread's markers (SessionEnd: the session's) before releasing pending claims, then on UserPromptSubmit syncs. The digest syncs first and adds the `Trunk:` line. Both hook syncs only run with a land section on trunk and `sync.auto` on, with a 12 s budget (`HOOK_SYNC_BUDGET_MS`), so the hook stays under 15 s.
- **`src/mcp.ts`** `hub_sync {mode?}`; **`src/human.ts`** `hub sync [--merge] [--recover]` (exit 1 when the sync couldn't do its job: skipped, conflict, needs_human); **`src/cli.ts`** HELP and `hub guard --pre-push`.
- **`src/guard.ts`** `prePushGuard`: refuses `refs/heads/<trunk>` (deletion included) and `<P>cand/…` (and the default `refs/agenthub/cand/…`), whatever the remote; land refs and everything else pass; `HUB_GUARD=off` bypasses it; a project without a land section (Phase 1) is left alone. **`src/init.ts`** installs it as `pre-push` through `guardHooks()`, chained like the others; the script keeps git's ref lines in a file in the git dir and makes them the chained hook's stdin (the file is unlinked once open).
- **`src/feed.ts`** (`FeedOptions.root`): notices for the agent's own landings (landed, failed, parked for an approval, in doubt, cancelled by someone else), for `landing.needs_approval` its human may give (evaluated on the landing's pending rules: `GET /landings/:id`, the `distinct` rule included), and for `trunk.moved` touching files it holds or has modified (committed since the merge base, or uncommitted); each said once, and dropped when the agent is shown that landing in that state anyway (`seenLanding`, called by `hub_wait {landing_id}`, `landing_status {landing_id}` and `land_cancel`). On `landing.landed` and `trunk.moved` a background `git fetch` of trunk (10 s, one at a time).
- **`src/format.ts`** `syncToolText`, `syncPromptText`, `syncDigestLine`, `syncNoticeKey`, `notices`; `DigestExtras.trunkLine`.

Decisions other steps and the integrator build on:

- **Sync needs a landing queue.** Without a land section on trunk (as last fetched; else the worktree's copy) the outcome is `skipped: no_queue` and the hooks don't sync at all, so Phase 1 projects never see their branches moved.
- **Skipped reasons** (`SyncSkipReason`): `no_queue`, `sync_running`, `edit_in_flight`, `fetch_failed`, `git_operation`, `detached_head`, `landing_in_flight`, `hub_unavailable`, `lander_paused` (replays only, design §3.8), `merges_in_branch`, `changed_meanwhile`, `timeout`, and in merge mode `dirty` and `merge_refused`. Each is tested in `test/sync.test.ts`.
- **Order inside step 6.** "Up to date" (trunk is an ancestor of HEAD) is checked before the dirty check, so a dirty worktree with nothing new is `up_to_date`, not `behind_dirty {behind: 0}`. A dirty worktree is reported `behind_dirty` even when the hub is down (it changes nothing); only a change of the worktree needs the hub's answer, and without it only the fast-forward goes ahead.
- **Staleness.** A lock or write-ahead record is abandoned when its pid is dead or it is older than 120 s (pid reuse); explicit syncs have a 90 s budget, so a live sync's lock is never taken for stale. A sync that ends in `needs_human` from a long-lived process (the MCP server) rewrites its record with pid 0, so pre-edit treats it as interrupted at once.
- **Settle says an unchanged state once.** `behind_dirty` and `conflict` for the same trunk tip (and the same forecast or commit) aren't repeated at every prompt (`G/agenthub/sync-said`); the digest records what it said too. Settle also prints `needs_human` (an interrupted sync with HEAD moved since), beyond the brief's "changed, conflicted or behind and dirty", because edits are blocked until a human looks.
- **Replays are unsigned.** The replay runs with `-c commit.gpgSign=false`: a signing prompt would hang a hook. The original commits stay in the backup ref, and the lander squashes them anyway.
- **Test fault.** `AGENTHUB_SYNC_FAULT=after-write-ahead|after-reset` with `NODE_ENV=test` SIGKILLs the syncing process there (the crash-recovery tests run the built `hub sync` as a child).
- **Old git.** Without `merge-tree --merge-base` (git < 2.40) the fast-forward and fully-landed cases still work; a replay or forecast fails with git's error and changes nothing (hooks stay quiet, `hub_sync` reports it); `hub doctor` already says so.
- **HOOKS_PATH_LINE and pre-push.** The "add this line to your hooks" note for a hooks directory hub init may not change still lists pre-commit, prepare-commit-msg, pre-merge-commit and pre-applypatch only: that one-liner would let the guard read the hook's stdin, which the user's pre-push needs. The pre-push guard is still written to `<common dir>/agenthub-hooks/pre-push` there.
- `test/guard-init.test.ts` and `test/mcp.test.ts` gained `pre-push` and `hub_sync` in their exact lists.
