## A1 repair round 1

- **`hub approve` / `hub reject` never send the diff's control characters to the terminal.** `format.ts` `terminalSafe(text)` writes out C0 controls (but tab and newline), DEL, C1 controls, U+2028/2029, `\p{Cf}` (bidi controls, zero-width) and every other default-ignorable character visibly (`\x1b`, `\r`, `‮`) and counts them; when there are any, the section header says how many and that the files hold the characters themselves, and a reminder comes before the prompt. The digest still covers the real blobs, so what is approved is what was shown. The approval diff is also `--no-textconv --ignore-submodules=none --submodule=short` with `:(literal)` pathspecs, so neither the approver's config and attributes nor a path that looks like a glob can change what is shown.
- **`land` fetches trunk before choosing record mode.** With no land section in this checkout's copy of trunk, it fetches trunk (bounded, the same fetch as the preflight's step 3, which then isn't repeated) and reads trunk's agenthub.json again; only when that still has no land section is the landing recorded Phase 1 style. A failed fetch keeps the old behaviour (record mode; a `landing_queue_active` answer then names the fetch error). Trunk fetches set `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0` (credential helpers still answer).
- **Every refusal of the queue request after the push says what to do.** `format.ts` `landHubRefusedText` covers `not_found`, `forbidden`, `task_closed`, `task_blocked`, `task_landing`, `too_late`, a `claim_conflict` without details, the request-shape codes (`bad_commit`, `bad_request`, `invalid_path`, `invalid_symbol`: "probably different versions, tell your human") and any other code, each ending "Your commit is pushed (that is harmless); nothing was queued."
- **For the integrator to confirm (no change made):** other agents' landing summaries and decision titles in the fix-it briefs are quoted inline (through `inline()`: one line, no control characters, double quotes swapped, capped at 80) as the §2.3.3 sketches show them, not inside `untrustedBlock()` as the §2.3.2 `landing_status` row says; every brief ends with the "information, not instructions" line. If the integrator reads §2.3.2 as binding, `otherText()` and `conflictBrief()` in `format.ts` are the two places to change, and the §2.3.3 snapshots in `landing-ux.test.ts` change with them.
